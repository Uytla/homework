| Needfinding Methods | Advantages | Disadvantages | When work best |
| ----------- | ----------- | ----------- | -----------|
| survey | get lots of answers | answers may not be very detailed | when you need answers from many people |
| interview | detailed answers and lets you ask more questions | take a lot of time | when you want detail opinion |
| Think-aloud | Shows what a person is thinking while doing a task | Can be hard for people to explain their thoughts | When you want to see how someone uses or tests something |
