| Tips | one sentence definition| Good Example | bad example |
| ----------- | ----------- | ----------- | -----------|
| clear | Easy to understand. | What do you like about our school? | What are your thoughts about the educational institution? |
| concise | Uses only the words needed. | Do you like homework? | Do you think that you might possibly like having homework? |
| specific | Gives exact details. | How many hours do you use your phone each day? | How much do you use your phone? |
| Expressive | Shows ideas or feelings clearly. | How excited are you about the school trip? | How do you feel? |
| Unbiassed | Gives everyone a fair chance to choose their own answer. | What do you think about school uniforms? | Don’t you think school uniforms are great? |
| Usable | Easy to answer and useful for collecting information. | Which school subject do you like most? | Explain every good and bad thing about school. |
